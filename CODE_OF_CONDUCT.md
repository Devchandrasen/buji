# Community expectations

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Help people reproduce problems and understand tradeoffs without personal attacks, harassment or discrimination.

Respect other people's privacy. Do not post private chats, credentials, personal files or identifying information without permission. Describe disagreements about code or evidence rather than making assumptions about contributors.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content and restrict participation when needed. Report a concern to the maintainer through an available private GitHub channel. Public issues should omit private details.

The project is maintained by [Devchandrasen](https://github.com/Devchandrasen). Participation follows these expectations and GitHub's community rules.
